Auberdine loop: how the route was found

Wayfinder's gathering loop out of Auberdine through Darkshore and back, worked out step by step: first the spots, then nearest neighbour builds a loop, then 2-opt uncrosses it until no swap helps.

The method

Spots. Darkshore's herbs and copper you can pick and still learn from (herbalism 88, mining 15): your own picks first, then classic spawn points; any two within map-% count as one. The 60 nearest the Auberdine mailbox make the loop.

Nearest neighbour. From the mailbox, always walk to the closest spot not yet visited; when none are left, walk home. Fast, but greedy: late in the loop it leaves long legs and crossings.

2-opt. Take any two legs, A→B and C→D. If A→C plus B→D is shorter, reverse everything between B and C: the two legs swap for two shorter ones, which is what uncrossing a crossing does. Keep going until no pair helps. Red dashes are the legs taken out, green the legs put in.
