# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-20)

- A see-through arrow points to the next waypoint and counts down the yards, across zones on the same continent.
- The arrow takes your class colour with a thin dark rim, and comes up to full while the mouse is on it.
- Night elves get a glowing wisp with circling motes instead of the arrow; `/wf wisp` switches for any race.
- Each step sets the game's own map pin, so the in-world marker shows too.
- Gather runs move on as you pass a spot or any of the next few, and loop back to the start.
- Trainer runs save where the trainer really stands and move on when you close their window.
- `/wf way <x> <y> [title]` adds your own waypoints; `/wf here` and a dropped map pin fix a step's position.
- Each run belongs to a character and only shows on it; each character keeps its own run and progress.
- Click the run name or right-click the compass-star minimap button to pick a run or start one over.
- Click the arrow and your race's worker answers; click too often and they file a grievance. `/wf voice` and `/wf sound`.
- A catch-up line builds a run of waiting errands, fitted to the time left in a dungeon or PvP queue.
- Queue waits are logged, so the estimate becomes the median of your own last waits.
- Hidden in arenas and battlegrounds, back afterwards.
- Buttons fit their text; text and buttons sit lower at rest and come up on mouseover.
- Runs for Chalice on WoW Forever: Cooking to 100, Gather: Teldrassil, Mine: Teldrassil, and the Auberdine loop of 60 herb and copper spots in Darkshore.
